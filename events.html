VIBELORE

Where Vibes Meet Vibrance

Aarohi is the annual cultural festival of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. It’s one of the largest cultural fests in Central India, attracting students from various colleges across the country. The event typically features a wide range of activities, including cultural competitions, workshops, guest lectures, concerts, and paper presentations.
