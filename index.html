Viva La Vida | Fest Theme: Night Life & Celebration

March 12–14, 2026 | Main Campus Ground | Register Now

Three nights. One campus. Every act you love.

Aarohi is VNIT's annual cultural festival — a run of concerts, competitions, art and celebration that turns the campus into a stage. This year's theme, Viva La Vida, brings a night-life energy of neon, cards and music to every corner of the ground.
