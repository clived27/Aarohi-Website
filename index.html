What is Aarohi?

Aarohi is the annual cultural festival of Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur. It's one of the largest cultural fests in Central India, attracting students from various colleges across the country!

When is Aarohi?

Aarohi takes place on 23–24–25 October, 2026. Stay tuned for details!
